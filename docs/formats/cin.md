# `.cin` — cutscene script (text, `cine_player.cpp`)

Case-insensitive `command(args)` lines, `;` comments, 20 objects. Commands: `endscene`, `loadgfx(file)`, `loadmbk(file)`, `playmusic(n)`, `stopmusic`, `setpos(obj,x,y)`, `setpic(obj,image)`, `setmove(obj,dx,dy,ticks)`, `settext(obj,stringId)`, `reset(obj)`, `resetall`, `setclip(x1,y1,x2,y2)`, `noclip`, `setanim(obj,speed,loop,images…)`, `setxwarp(obj,from,to)`, `setywarp(obj,from,to)`, `stopanim(obj)`, `renderscene(frames)`; `setbackcolor(layer,c)` and `setclipcolor(layer,c)` set a layer's colour, black for `c` = 0, white otherwise; the screen is cleared with layer 0's back colour and the bars outside `setclip` are filled with its clip colour (the white flashes of `intro.cin` and `end.cin`, D-95). `setalpha`, `setdeltaalpha`, `setvolume`, `setdeltavolume` are accepted and ignored: the 2002 player computed the fade and the volume but drew neither (its alpha blend was already commented out), and no shipped cutscene uses them (D-19). Esc skips.
